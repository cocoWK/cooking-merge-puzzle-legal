Who we are

Cooking Merge Puzzle is a mobile game published by wangkunyu, an individual developer. This policy explains what information the Android app handles. The package name is com.cooking.merge.puzzle.

Information the game handles

The game does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.

  • Game progress on your device. Level, energy, settings such as music, sound, vibration and language, and whether you have removed ads, are stored locally on the device.
  • Google Play Games cloud save. If you are signed in to Play Games, that local progress can be uploaded so it can be restored after you reinstall the app. Google processes that sign-in under its own terms.
  • Purchases. The optional “remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We receive the purchase result for the product removeads_1.99. We do not receive your card number or full payment details.
  • Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including banners, interstitials and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may use an advertising identifier, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. If you buy remove-ads, obligatory ads are turned off. Rewarded ads stay optional.
  • Attribution. The app uses AppsFlyer to understand installs and in-app events, such as whether the app was opened from a link. AppsFlyer may receive device and advertising identifiers for that purpose.

How we use it

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings.
  • To restore progress through Google Play Games when you are signed in.
  • To complete and remember a remove-ads purchase.
  • To show ads and measure whether an install or event came from a campaign.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the services named above, under their own policies:

  • Google Play and Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • AppsFlyer: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/

Your choices

  • You can limit ad personalization in your device’s Google settings (Ads or Privacy).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.
  • You can sign out of Play Games or delete Play Games data from your Google account settings. Local progress stays on the device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the local copy of the game data.

Children

Cooking Merge Puzzle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and security

Local data stays on the device until you clear the app storage or uninstall it. Cloud progress stays in Google Play Games until you delete it there or it is overwritten by a newer save. Purchase records are kept by Google Play according to Google’s rules. We rely on Google Play, the device operating system, and the third parties above to protect data in transit and at rest.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

游戏会处理的信息

  • 归因。 应用使用 AppsFlyer 了解安装和游戏内事件，例如是否从某个链接打开。AppsFlyer 可能为此接收设备和广告标识符。
